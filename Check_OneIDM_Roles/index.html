Check Roles Availability in OneIDM

Follow the steps below to check if the required roles are available for your account in OneIDM.

Step 1: Check PI0311 Roles

  • Go to OneIDM using the link: https://oneidm.bosch.com/oneidm/html/rbwebshop/#/start/itshop/VI_Start
  • Click Requests → Extend or Revoke
  • In the IT Application column, search PI0311
  • In Services column, search for first role: X:H5TM_XXXX_MIN_USER and click Search
  • To search the second role, enter X:H5TM_XXXX_RBEI_EMPLOYEE and click Search

Step 2: Check PXG011 System Role

  • In IT Application column, search PXG011
  • In Services column, enter the role: X:0GHR_FIORI_TRV_RBEI_PI0 and click Search

Step 3: Check Portal System Role

  • In IT Application column, search Portal
  • In Services column, enter the role: CN=rb_flp_swift_trv_grp,OU=portal,OU=groups,OU=esi,DC=wam-bosch,DC=com and click Search

Role Verification for IT Applications – PH1011, PXG011, and Portal

You will need to verify whether roles are assigned for the following IT applications: PH1011, PXG011, and Portal.

Step 1: Open OneIDM

Click on the OneIDM link below:

https://oneidm.bosch.com/oneidm/html/rbwebshop/#/start/itshop/VI_Start

Then follow these steps:

  • Go to Requests → Extend or Revoke
  • In the IT Application column, search for the required application name.
  • In the Services column, click the filter icon, enter the Role Name, and click Search.

Step 2: Check PH1011 Roles

Search for the following roles under PH1011:

X:H0SY_XXXX_ALL_M_MINUSER_HR
X:H0HR_INXX_MA_ESS

If PH1011 roles are available, proceed to check for Default Roles in the PXG011 system.

Step 3: Check PXG011 Roles

Look for these roles under PXG011:

  • X:0GFI_FIORI_SSF_MYACC_PDX
  • X:0GXX_FIORI_ALL_MINUSER
  • X:0GHR_FIORI_HCM_IN_HPE_PH1
  • X:0GHR_FIORI_HCM_IN_ESS_PH1
  • X:0GHR_FIORI_TRV_RBEI_PI0

If PXG011 roles are found, move on to check Default Roles in the Portal system.

Step 4: Check Portal Roles

Verify the following Portal roles are assigned:

  • CN=org_every_rbei_associate,OU=portal,OU=groups,OU=esi,DC=wam-bosch,DC=com
  • CN=rb_myhr_associate_in,OU=portal,OU=groups,OU=esi,DC=wam-bosch,DC=com
  • CN=rb_fi_myaccounting_self_service_new_ui,OU=portal,OU=groups,OU=esi,DC=wam-bosch,DC=com
  • CN=rb_fi_myaccounting_self_service,OU=portal,OU=groups,OU=esi,DC=wam-bosch,DC=com
  • CN=rb_flp_hr_core_users,OU=portal,OU=groups,OU=esi,DC=wam-bosch,DC=com
  • CN=rb_flp_swift_trv_grp,OU=portal,OU=groups,OU=esi,DC=wam-bosch,DC=com

Conclusion

Once you have verified all roles across PH1011, PXG011, and Portal, document your findings and proceed with any required role extensions or revocations as per the OneIDM guidelines.
